## Find a subject to explore

Our current starter bank has 106 prompts across a mix of subjects. These guides list the prompts actually present, explain common interpretation errors and connect you to the mixed Unlimited game. Category pages do not claim to provide subject-only play.

### Geography

Oceans, continents, countries, Great Lakes and Pacific US states. Read geographic qualifiers carefully and distinguish an island from a sovereign state. [Explore geography prompts](/categories/geography/).

### Sports

The sports collection covers playing positions, tennis tournaments, swimming strokes, athletics events, gymnastics apparatus, fencing weapons and NBA league MVP winners. [Explore sports prompts](/categories/sports/).

### Science

Planets, chemical symbols, noble gases, alkali metals, SI units and Platonic solids. The condition in the question often matters as much as the subject. [Explore science prompts](/categories/science/).

## More subjects in mixed dives

Starter prompts for food, animals, entertainment, art, mathematics and general knowledge now have their own collection page, alongside calendars, language and games.

### Music

Standard guitar tuning has six strings but five distinct note letters. Learn how to answer the actual prompt rather than naming a song or instrument. [Explore music prompts](/categories/music/).

### Language

Greek letter names and Roman numeral symbols use different answer formats. Review the accepted scope and the examples already in our bank. [Explore language prompts](/categories/language/).

### Games

Chess piece types and playing-card suits reward careful reading. A king fits the chess prompt, while hearts is a card suit. [Explore games prompts](/categories/games/).


### Calendar

Weekdays and month lengths have exact conditions. Three existing prompts cover days of the week, 30-day months and 31-day months. [Explore Calendar prompts](/categories/calendar/).
