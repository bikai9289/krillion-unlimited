A public support email is not available yet. Direct email submissions for the topics below are not currently available. Please do not send messages to an unconfirmed address.

## Bug reports

Describe what happened and what you expected. Include the page URL, browser, device, approximate time and steps to reproduce. Screenshots can help, but remove personal information. Never send cookies, passwords or other credentials.

## Answer corrections

Include the exact prompt, the answer you entered and why it should be accepted. Add a reliable supporting source where possible. We review our own question bank; we cannot change answers or results on krillion.io or other sites.

## Advertising and partnerships

Describe your organization, proposed placement or partnership and a public website. Advertising must remain clearly separate from gameplay and cannot influence accepted answers or scores.

## Privacy requests

Explain the information or browser-held progress your request concerns. Do not email your player cookie or raw browser identifier. We may need further details to locate a record safely. Clearing local browser data does not delete server records; see our privacy policy for storage details.
