## A different kind of trivia

A Krillion-style game asks for one answer to an open prompt. Many responses can be correct, but each prepared entry has a rarity tier. Our independent version turns points into an ocean depth: every point is ten metres, up to 7,000 metres for a perfect seven-question dive.

## Daily or Unlimited?

Daily saves one result per browser per New York day and gives everyone the same seven prompts. Unlimited starts a fresh mixed set and has no daily play cap. Both use the same matching engine, 25-second answer window and six-tier scoring.

## What happens in a dive

Click Play Now, read the preview, and type one valid answer. An unknown entry can be retried while time remains. A proposed spelling correction needs a second submission to confirm. Passing or timing out gives zero points for that round. After seven rounds, the report opens your accepted-answer review.

## What this version does differently

We use our own small curated question bank rather than official daily puzzle data. Scores and timing are validated on the server. Your browser can restore the current dive using an essential player cookie and a local run pointer. There is no account or payment requirement. Rankings show the best completed result per browser in the UTC day or week.
