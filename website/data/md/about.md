## Made for curious players

Krillion Unlimited is a free independent rare-answer trivia game. Each dive contains seven open prompts and a 25-second window per answer. Your prepared score becomes ocean depth.

## Our question bank

This version uses its own independently assembled starter bank. It does not fetch official questions or answer lists from krillion.io. New run snapshots use observed answer frequency after a prompt reaches 100 accepted browser choices over 30 days. Smaller samples use labelled editorial fallback. Saved Daily editions keep their scoring for that date. The bank is limited and does not recognize every valid response.

## Daily, Unlimited and rankings

Daily has one saved result per browser and follows midnight in New York. Unlimited can be replayed. Anonymous rankings use this service’s completed games and show one best result per browser in each UTC day or Monday-based week.

## Independence

We are not affiliated with or endorsed by krillion.io. Our daily puzzle, accepted-answer lists and statistics are our own. There are no paid plans, sign-in accounts or multiplayer features.
