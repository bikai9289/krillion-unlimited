# Name one of the four main compass directions

North, South, East and West are the four accepted answers in this site's current question bank. Enter one full direction, rather than all four together. The prompt asks for a member of a set, so a single correct choice is enough. Northeast and southwest are useful directions too, but they belong to a different group from the four main directions requested here.

This guide covers our independent game. The same wording on another trivia website may use a different answer list or scoring system. The answer review saved with your completed dive is the right reference for the points awarded in that particular run.

## Read “four main” as the important restriction

The key task is to distinguish a cardinal direction from an intermediate direction. North, east, south and west form the cardinal set. Northeast lies between north and east, while southwest lies between south and west. Those combined names provide more detail, but more detail does not make them better answers to this specific question.

A useful test is to picture a simple compass rose with just four labels. Start at the top with north, then move clockwise through east, south and west. If the answer you are considering needs a position between two of those labels, it is outside the set requested by this prompt. That quick picture is usually more useful than searching for an unusual word.

## A map exercise that makes the distinction practical

Imagine a small map with a library in the centre, a park directly above it, a station directly below it, a school to its right and a museum to its left. If the map is drawn with north at the top, the park is north of the library, the station is south, the school is east and the museum is west.

Now place a café above and to the right of the library. Its position is northeast. That would answer a question asking for an intermediate compass direction, but it would not answer our current four-direction prompt. The exercise shows why a geographically meaningful response can still fail an exact category condition.

The phrase “north at the top” matters. A map can be rotated, and a diagram should identify its orientation before you infer direction from the page. The trivia question avoids that complication by asking for a direction name, not the direction of an object on an unidentified map.

## Why left, right, up and down are different answers

Left and right depend on which way a person is facing. If you turn around, your left side changes relative to the room. Cardinal directions describe orientation independently of that turn. A doorway does not become a different compass direction from a table just because you turn your chair.

Up and down can describe vertical movement, positions on a page or a screen layout. Neither word is a substitute for north or south in this answer bank. Similarly, “toward the station” may be a perfectly useful instruction in conversation, but it does not name one of the four requested directions.

This is a recurring trivia skill. Identify the kind of answer the prompt requests before choosing an example. A direction name, a landmark and a relative instruction might all help someone navigate, yet they are not interchangeable answer types.

## What to type in this game

Use North, South, East or West. Capitalization does not affect matching. The current bank does not configure N, S, E or W as aliases for this prompt, so the full word is the safest input. An abbreviation that appears on a compass is not automatically a supported game alias.

Enter only one answer. Writing “north or east” introduces extra text and does not ask the matcher to select whichever part it recognizes. If you make a spelling mistake and receive a proposed correction, inspect the suggestion and submit again to confirm it before the timer expires. A displayed suggestion alone is not a scored answer.

## Prepared points are not a geography fact

The fallback bank assigns North to Plankton, South to Schooler, East to Rare and West to One in a Krillion. With the configured tier values, those fallback entries correspond to 10, 30, 60 and 100 points. These are game settings, not evidence that one direction is intrinsically harder or less important.

New runs can use observed choices once a prompt has enough eligible browser choices under the scoring rules. A saved run keeps its own scoring snapshot. Consequently, do not use a static guide as a promise that West must always receive the same points in every future dive. Check the run's review when you want to understand an awarded score.

## Try these checks before your next dive

First, decide whether “southeast” belongs to the requested four. It does not: it combines two cardinal names. Next, decide whether “west” remains a cardinal direction if a map is rotated. It does; rotating the page changes its appearance, not the category of the word. Finally, decide whether the letter W is a guaranteed accepted submission. It is a conventional label, but this bank does not list it as an alias.

When the question appears in a timed dive, you only need one full direction you can spell confidently. Practising the distinction is useful beyond this easy example: restrictive words such as “main,” “standard,” “classic” and “land” often determine the answer set in other prompts.
